# Architecture decision records

An ADR captures a consequential choice, its forces, and its implications. It is not a retrospective success claim.

```markdown
# ADR-NNN: Choose <decision>

## Status
Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNN

## Context
Constraints, evidence, and alternatives that force a decision.

## Decision
The chosen boundary and why it fits the evidence.

## Consequences
What becomes easier, harder, irreversible, or operationally required.

## Verification
How the decision will be tested or revisited.
```

Use exact repository evidence for measurements. If data is unavailable, state the uncertainty and decision criterion instead of inventing percentages. Link superseding ADRs both ways and preserve prior records rather than rewriting history.
